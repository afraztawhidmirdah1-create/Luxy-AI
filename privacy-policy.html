Luxy AI Privacy Policy

Luxy AI is made by Afraz Studio. Last updated: October 2026.

What stays on your phone

Your chat history, notes, to-do list and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that store them and there is no account or sign-in.

What is sent over the internet

To answer your questions, the text you type (and any photo or file you choose to attach) is sent to third-party services that provide AI answers, web knowledge, news, weather and currency rates. If you ask for weather, your city or approximate location is sent to the weather service. We do not sell your data and the app shows no ads.

Google account and backup

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address and profile picture to show your account. Your backup (chats, notes and tasks without attached photos) is saved in the private app-data area of your own Google Drive, which only this app can read. You can sign out at any time and delete the backup from your Google account settings.

Images, videos and voice

Your image and video descriptions are sent to a third-party image generation service. Voice reading uses your phone's built-in speech engine. Voice input uses your phone's speech recognition service and only runs when you tap the microphone.

Permissions

Internet: to get answers. Camera: only when you choose to take a photo. Microphone: only when you tap the mic for voice input. Google account: optional; if you sign in, a backup of your chats is saved in your own Google Drive app data. Microphone: only when you tap the mic button. Location: optional, only for local weather.

Children

Luxy AI is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete chats inside the app, or clear the app data or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device.
